WEARHOUSE — INVENTORY EDITOR UPDATE

New: Edit item allows administrators to edit the product and manage its serial numbers
within the SAME edit dialog. Quantity updates to reflect serialized physical stock.

- Change product quantity: increasing quantity adds serial input fields.
  Each new physical unit MUST have its own serial number.
- To reduce quantity, remove the exact unused serials with the × buttons.
- Existing unused serials can be renamed.
- Serial numbers with delivery history, or reserved/out/delivered serials,
  are protected and shown as read-only to preserve audit records.
- Serial edits, quantity and product details are saved in one transaction.
- Existing glassmorphism theme and delivery-order workflows are unchanged.

DATABASE
The new manager-only Supabase function admin_update_product_and_serials is ALREADY
installed in the connected Trade database and verified. You do NOT need to
